What it does

Everything a structural
engineer needs in one tab.

No installation. No subscription. No server. Just open it in your browser and start analysing.

📐

Real Beam Analysis

Cantilever, simply supported, and fixed-fixed beams with full deflection, bending moment, and shear force diagrams rendered live as you adjust parameters.

🏗️

Standard Section Library

Choose from Universal Beams, Universal Columns, Rectangular Hollow Sections, and Circular Hollow Sections — or input custom parametric dimensions.

⚖️

KS 02-104 Compliance

Automatic code checks against Kenya's structural steel standard: deflection limits, bending stress, shear capacity, safety factor, and section adequacy — all at once.

✦ AI

AI Engineering Assistant

Powered by Claude. Ask it why your beam is failing, get section recommendations, or request a plain-English explanation of any compliance result.

💰

Live KES Cost Estimator

Material cost calculated in Kenyan Shillings based on your section geometry and current steel pricing. Adjust the price per kg in real time.

📄

PDF Report Export

Generate a professional structural analysis report with diagrams, compliance stamps, cost breakdown, and input parameters — ready to share with a client or supervising engineer.

Workflow

From inputs to report
in under a minute.

01

Choose your beam configuration

Select cantilever, simply supported, or fixed-fixed. Pick a load type — point load, UDL, or triangular — and set its position along the span.

02

Define your cross section

Use a parametric section or pick from the standard library (UB, UC, RHS, CHS). Set material properties and include self-weight if needed.

03

Read the live results

Deflection, bending moment, shear force, stress, and safety factor update instantly. The KS 02-104 compliance panel tells you exactly what passes and what fails.

04

Export your report

One click generates a printable PDF with your diagrams, all results, compliance stamps, and a KES material cost breakdown.

Code compliance

Built around
Kenyan standards.

Most structural software is calibrated for US or European codes. StructaViz is built ground-up for the Kenyan context — referencing KS 02-104, BS 5950, and Eurocode 3 where relevant to local practice.
