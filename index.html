Features

Analyse, check and report without leaving the browser

Everything needed to take a member from first sketch to a printed result.

Beam analysis

Cantilever, simply supported and fixed-fixed beams, with one to three continuous spans. Apply point, uniformly distributed and triangular loads.

Live diagrams

Deflection, bending moment and shear force diagrams update as you change the design. Switch to the 3D and section views to inspect the member.

Section library

Choose standard UB, UC, RHS and CHS sections, or define a custom rectangular, I, circular or hollow box section.

KS 02-104 checks

Deflection, bending stress, shear capacity and safety factor are checked against KS 02-104, with a clear pass or fail for each.

Two-way slab check

BS 8110 checks for slabs simply supported on four sides: bending moments, span-to-depth deflection, minimum thickness and required reinforcement in each direction.

Report and cost

Export a PDF report of the design and its results, and estimate the material cost from your own price per kilogram.
