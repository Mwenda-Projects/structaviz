Mass per metre = d² / 162.2 kg (about 7850 kg/m³ steel).

Handy equivalents

1 kPa = 1 kN/m². 1 MPa = 1 N/mm². 1 kN/m³ ≈ 102 kg/m³ of weight. 1 m³ of water ≈ 1 tonne ≈ 9.81 kN. Concrete is about 24 kN/m³ and steel about 78.5 kN/m³. A cube strength is roughly 1.25 times the cylinder strength.
